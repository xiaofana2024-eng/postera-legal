Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Postera is a local stamp collection and condition archive app. This policy explains how Postera handles information.

Data we store on your device

Specimen records, photos you attach, voice memo text, inventory progress, and optional in-app purchase credits are stored locally on your device. Postera does not require an account.

Permissions

  • Camera, microphone, photo library, and speech recognition are requested only when you use the related feature.
  • App Tracking Transparency and notifications may be requested once when you first open the home screen. Postera does not use tracking identifiers for advertising measurement in product flows and does not send push notifications after the prompt.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. Postera stores purchase product identifiers and a local credit balance on device for feature unlocks.

Hosting of this page

This policy page is hosted as a static site. Standard web server logs may be collected by the host.

Contact

For privacy questions about Postera, contact the developer through the App Store listing support channel once published.
